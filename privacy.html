Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026

Bisaya is designed to work without an account and without collecting personal data.

Data collection

Bisaya does not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location, or advertising identifiers, and it does not contain advertising or track you across other apps or websites.

Anonymous usage statistics

To understand how the app is used and improve it, Bisaya sends anonymous usage events to our analytics provider, Mixpanel. These events record things like opening the app, starting and finishing a lesson, which words you answered and whether each answer was correct, units completed, time spent in a lesson, and your level, streak, and number of words learned. Each install is identified only by a random ID created by the app; it is not linked to your identity, Apple ID, or advertising identifier. Mixpanel also receives your device’s IP address when events are sent, which it uses to estimate an approximate city and country and does not store.

You can turn this off at any time in Settings by switching off Share usage stats. When it is off, Bisaya stops recording usage events, deletes any it has not sent yet, and replaces the random ID with a new one so nothing sent later can be linked to it. A batch that was already being sent when you switched it off (for example, retrying after a lost connection) may still finish sending.

We do not sell this data or use it for advertising. It is not shared with anyone else except Mixpanel as our service provider.

Information stored on your device

Your vocabulary progress, review schedule, journey progress, daily goal history, and preferences are stored locally on your device. This information is used only to provide the app’s learning features.

Progress exports

You may choose to export a JSON copy of your learning progress from Settings. The file is created locally and is shared only with the destination you select through your device’s system share sheet. Bisaya does not receive a copy.

Notifications

If you allow reminders, notification scheduling is handled locally by your device. Bisaya does not operate a notification server and does not receive notification identifiers.

Children

Bisaya does not collect personal information and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Usage statistics are anonymous and can be turned off in Settings.

Changes

If the app’s data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before the change is released.

Contact

For privacy questions, visit the Bisaya support page.
